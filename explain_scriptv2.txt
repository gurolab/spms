====================================================================
VERSION A: 10-MINUTE TIMED REHEARSAL SCRIPT
====================================================================

Goal:
- Fast, clear, executive-level walkthrough for mixed technical and non-technical stakeholders.

Demo order:
- Employee -> Supply Officer -> Inventory Officer -> Property Custodian -> Auditor -> System Admin

--------------------------------------------------------------------
0:00 - 0:45 | Opening
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Say:
"Good day everyone. This is my capstone system, SMS or Supply Management
System for Cotabato State University.

The project addresses manual and Excel-based delays in requests, inventory
monitoring, report preparation, and property accountability."

--------------------------------------------------------------------
0:45 - 1:20 | Non-IT Illustration
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Say:
"Think of SMS as one secure university service desk with six windows:
Employee, Supply, Inventory, Property, Auditor, and Admin.

Requests enter once, then updates flow across issuance, inventory, property
accountability, and audit reports in one centralized record."

--------------------------------------------------------------------
1:20 - 2:20 | Employee Role
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Open:
- employee/dashboard.php
- employee/requisition.php
- employee/request-details.php
- employee/acknowledgment.php

Say:
"As an employee, I create a request, add line items, and track status.
While it is still Requested, edits are allowed. Once processing starts, the
request is locked for integrity. This supports transparent on-time requests."

--------------------------------------------------------------------
2:20 - 3:20 | Supply Officer Role
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Open:
- supplyofficer/index.php
- supplyofficer/receive.php
- supplyofficer/ris.php
- supplyofficer/issue_supplies.php

Say:
"As Supply Officer, I record deliveries, monitor RIS requests, and issue
supplies. Received and issued quantities update stock records and transaction
history, reducing manual errors and improving traceability."

--------------------------------------------------------------------
3:20 - 4:20 | Inventory Officer Role
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Open:
- inventoryofficer/dashboard.php
- inventoryofficer/stock_cards.php
- inventoryofficer/slc.php
- inventoryofficer/inventory_reconciliation.php

Say:
"As Inventory Officer, I monitor stock health, reorder levels, and movement
history. Reconciliation identifies items that need replenishment action and
supports evidence-based planning."

--------------------------------------------------------------------
4:20 - 5:20 | Property Custodian Role
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Open:
- propertycustodian/dashboard.php
- propertycustodian/ics.php
- propertycustodian/par.php
- propertycustodian/property_cards.php
- propertycustodian/property_transfers.php

Say:
"As Property Custodian, I manage non-consumable asset accountability through
ICS, PAR, property cards, and transfer records. This creates a complete
assignment-to-transfer accountability chain."

--------------------------------------------------------------------
5:20 - 6:20 | Auditor Role
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Open:
- auditor/dashboard.php
- administrator/physical_inventory_reports.php
- administrator/physical_ppe_reports.php
- administrator/unserviceable_reports.php
- administrator/audit_logs.php

Say:
"As Auditor, I review RPCI, RPCPPE, IIRUP, and scoped audit logs.
The system shows who did what, when, and on which record, improving compliance
review and evidence retrieval."

--------------------------------------------------------------------
6:20 - 7:20 | System Admin Role
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Open:
- administrator/dashboard.php
- administrator/users.php
- administrator/roles.php
- administrator/departments.php
- administrator/suppliers.php
- administrator/system_settings.php

Say:
"As System Admin, I manage users, roles, departments, settings, and master
data. Role-based access ensures each office only sees authorized modules."

--------------------------------------------------------------------
7:20 - 8:40 | Evaluation Variables (from Chapter I)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Say:
"Project evaluation focuses on four variables:
Perceived Usefulness, Perceived Ease of Use, Accessibility, and Security.

Usefulness: less delay and better tracking.
Ease of Use: role-focused pages and guided forms.
Accessibility: intranet-oriented access within project scope.
Security: role guards, CSRF protection, and audit logging."

--------------------------------------------------------------------
8:40 - 9:30 | Scope and Limitation Disclosure
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Say:
"Consistent with the documented scope, the current version is mainly for
institutional network use, and legacy manual records are still encoded manually.
Planned next steps include remote access hardening, data import tools, and
barcode and forecasting enhancements."

--------------------------------------------------------------------
9:30 - 10:00 | Closing
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Say:
"In summary, SMS centralizes supply and property operations, improves
accountability, and supports faster, data-driven decisions. Thank you, and I
welcome your questions and recommendations."

VERSION B: 20-MINUTE TIMED REHEARSAL SCRIPT
====================================================================

Goal:
- Full walkthrough with stronger project-study alignment for adviser, chairperson,
offices, auditor, and SMS Office stakeholders.

Demo order:
- Employee -> Supply Officer -> Inventory Officer -> Property Custodian -> Auditor -> System Admin

--------------------------------------------------------------------
0:00 - 1:30 | Opening and Context
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Say:
"Good day everyone. I am presenting my capstone project, the Supply and
Property Management System or SMS for Cotabato State University.

The system addresses manual process issues in requests, inventory control,
property accountability, and report generation."

--------------------------------------------------------------------
1:30 - 3:00 | Problem Statement and Objective Mapping
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Say:
"Based on Chapter I, the primary problems are:
1) slow manual recording and retrieval,
2) inventory inaccuracies and stock issues,
3) time-consuming report generation.

The objective is a web-based system that centralizes recording, tracking,
reporting, and role-based transactions."

--------------------------------------------------------------------
3:00 - 4:00 | Simple Illustration for Non-IT Stakeholders
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Say:
"Imagine one official digital logbook shared by six offices.
Each office has a dedicated window but writes into one secure institutional
record. This removes duplicate encoding and supports real-time visibility."

--------------------------------------------------------------------
4:00 - 6:00 | Employee Walkthrough
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Open:
- employee/dashboard.php
- employee/requisition.php
- employee/request-details.php
- employee/acknowledgment.php

Say:
"Employee users submit RIS requests with purpose and item lines.
Editable while Requested, then locked once processing begins.
Status monitoring gives transparency and reduces manual follow-up."

Illustration line:
"Before: a paper request could be misplaced.
Now: request status is visible in one click."

--------------------------------------------------------------------
6:00 - 8:00 | Supply Officer Walkthrough
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Open:
- supplyofficer/index.php
- supplyofficer/receive.php
- supplyofficer/ris.php
- supplyofficer/issue_supplies.php
- supplyofficer/inventory_reports.php

Say:
"Supply Officer records received deliveries, monitors RIS queue, and issues
quantities based on stock and request balance. Transactions are recorded and
stock updates are reflected in system records."

Illustration line:
"Before: encoding and checking were separate manual steps.
Now: receiving and issuing are linked to stock records."

--------------------------------------------------------------------
8:00 - 10:00 | Inventory Officer Walkthrough
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Open:
- inventoryofficer/dashboard.php
- inventoryofficer/stock_cards.php
- inventoryofficer/slc.php
- inventoryofficer/inventory_reconciliation.php

Say:
"Inventory Officer uses stock cards and supply ledger to monitor balances and
movement history. Reconciliation highlights items requiring replenishment."

Illustration line:
"Before: shortage is noticed late.
Now: reorder indicators support earlier planning."

--------------------------------------------------------------------
10:00 - 12:00 | Property Custodian Walkthrough
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Open:
- propertycustodian/dashboard.php
- propertycustodian/ics.php
- propertycustodian/par.php
- propertycustodian/property_cards.php
- propertycustodian/property_transfers.php

Say:
"Property Custodian manages accountability documents for non-consumable assets:
ICS, PAR, property cards, and transfers.
This preserves assignment, movement, and transfer history."

Illustration line:
"Before: tracing accountability takes multiple files.
Now: accountability chain is centralized."

--------------------------------------------------------------------
12:00 - 14:00 | Auditor Walkthrough
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Open:
- auditor/dashboard.php
- administrator/physical_inventory_reports.php
- administrator/physical_ppe_reports.php
- administrator/unserviceable_reports.php
- administrator/audit_logs.php

Say:
"Auditor role reviews compliance reports and scoped activity logs.
RPCI, RPCPPE, and IIRUP records are reviewable with supporting details.
Audit logs provide actor, action, timestamp, and entity references."

Illustration line:
"Before: evidence gathering is manual and fragmented.
Now: compliance evidence is easier to retrieve."

--------------------------------------------------------------------
14:00 - 15:30 | System Admin Walkthrough
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Open:
- administrator/dashboard.php
- administrator/users.php
- administrator/roles.php
- administrator/departments.php
- administrator/suppliers.php
- administrator/system_settings.php
- administrator/audit_logs.php

Say:
"System Admin manages accounts, roles, departments, suppliers, and governance
settings. Role-based access enforces module boundaries while preserving full
oversight for administration."

--------------------------------------------------------------------
15:30 - 17:30 | Evaluation Summary (Chapter I Variables)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Say:
"Evaluation variables are:
1) Perceived Usefulness
2) Perceived Ease of Use
3) Accessibility
4) Security

Usefulness: workflow delays are reduced.
Ease of Use: role-specific pages simplify user tasks.
Accessibility: designed for authorized institutional use.
Security: role guards, CSRF checks, and audit logs are implemented."

--------------------------------------------------------------------
17:30 - 18:45 | Scope, Limitations, and Honest Disclosure
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Say:
"Current scope is primarily institutional network deployment.
Legacy data migration remains largely manual.
These limitations are documented and considered future enhancement areas."

--------------------------------------------------------------------
18:45 - 19:30 | Roadmap
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Say:
"Recommended next steps:
1) remote access hardening,
2) import tools for legacy records,
3) barcode integration,
4) inventory forecasting,
5) continuous user training and feedback."

--------------------------------------------------------------------
19:30 - 20:00 | Closing
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Say:
"SMS delivers centralized, accountable, and role-based supply and property
management aligned with project objectives. Thank you, and I welcome your
questions."
